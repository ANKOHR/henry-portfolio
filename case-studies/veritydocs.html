The problem

Invoices, contracts, and statements arrive in different formats. Re-keying them creates delays and arithmetic errors, while extracted values without source evidence are difficult to review.

The solution

VerityDocs extracts candidate fields and keeps provenance so a reviewer can trace a value to its source. A deterministic FIN-001 check compares financial totals, while confidence and review paths keep uncertain results visible to a person.

Measured outcome

What was verified.

The build has 49 backend tests. In the FIN-001 example, £8,000 plus £1,600 validates to £9,600. A stated total of £9,900 is flagged as a failure. A live demo is available.
