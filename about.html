About

Evidence over credentials.

I am 17 and in Year 13, studying Maths, Physics, and German. I build technical projects around school hours and make the work reviewable through source code, tests, measurements, and clear limits.

I care about useful systems that solve real operational problems, not demos that overstate what they can do.

My work is scoped, tested, and evidence-backed. I prefer predictable systems, clear audit trails, and keeping people in control of consequential actions.
